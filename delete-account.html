Delete your PepForge account

You can permanently delete your PepForge account and all the data associated with it. You can do this in the app, or by email if you no longer have the app.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open PepForge and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email

Use this option if you have uninstalled the app, cannot sign in, or prefer not to use the app.

  1. Send an email to hello@pepforge.app with the subject "Delete my PepForge account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to PepForge. This is how we confirm the account is yours. If you can no longer use that address, tell us and we will ask for other confirmation.
  3. We will confirm by email before deleting, then delete the account and tell you when it is done. We act on requests within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

Never send us your password.

What is deleted

Everything tied to your account, permanently and in a single step:

  • your account: email address, name, password hash and sign-in sessions;
  • all dose records, plans, schedules, protocols and templates;
  • blood-work results and reference ranges, body measurements and symptoms;
  • vials, syringes, ratings and saved calculations;
  • progress-photo details stored on our server (the images themselves were never uploaded);
  • AI assistant conversations;
  • API tokens and pending changes proposed by connected apps;
  • your preferences, and the internal account event records you created.

This cannot be undone. If you want to keep a copy, ask us for an export first (see the privacy policy).

What is kept, and for how long

  • Administrator action records: if an administrator ever took an action on your account (for example, changing your plan), we keep a record that the action happened, with every stored value about you removed. These records hold no health data and no email address.
  • Data on your phone: deleting your account does not reach into your phone. Uninstall the app, or sign out, to remove the copy it holds.

LEAD: if Railway database backups are enabled, add here how long deleted data can remain in backups.

Only want to delete your data, not your account?

You can delete individual entries in the app at any time. To erase all your entries at once but keep your account, use "Clear my data" in the companion web app's settings, or email us. Deleted entries are erased either immediately or within 30 days, depending on the type of entry. The privacy policy explains which.
