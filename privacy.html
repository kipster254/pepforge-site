Privacy policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data PepForge collects, why, where it is kept, who else handles it, how long it is kept, and how you can see, export, correct or delete it. It is written in plain English. If anything is unclear, contact us at hello@pepforge.app.

The short version: you enter health information about yourself, so we treat all of it as sensitive. We use it only to run PepForge for you. We do not sell it, we show no ads, and we use no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. A small number of service providers handle it on our behalf, listed in section 5. You can delete your entries or your whole account.

1. Who we are

PepForge is a personal tracking app for adults. It is operated by Kiplagat Leting, an individual based in Thailand ("we", "us"), who is responsible for your personal data (the data controller).

This policy covers the PepForge Android app, the PepForge server it syncs with, and this website. Your PepForge account is also used by our companion web app, which runs on the same server; this policy covers your data there too.

PepForge is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It is not a healthcare provider, and we do not give medical advice.

2. Data we collect

We collect only what you give us and what is needed to run your account. Most of it is health information you choose to enter.

Account

  • Your email address and, if you give one, your name.
  • Your password. We never store it in readable form, only a one-way hash.
  • Account status details: when the account was created, when you last signed in, your plan and your role.

Confirmations you give when you start

  • The date and time you confirmed you are 18 or older. We do not ask for your date of birth.
  • The date and time you accepted the disclaimer, and which version of it you accepted.
  • The date and time you finished onboarding.

Health and activity information you enter

  • Dose records: injections, oral doses and peptide doses. These include what was taken, the amount, the date and time, and any details you add, such as injection site, needle, batch reference or notes.
  • Plans: the schedules and protocols you create, the changes you make to them, and templates you save.
  • Supplies and calculations: vials, syringes and the calculator results you choose to save.
  • Your own entries for substances you add yourself, and your ratings.
  • Symptoms you log.
  • Blood work results, and any personal reference ranges you set for them.
  • Body measurements, such as weight and body composition.

Progress photos

Where the app offers progress photos, the images stay on your phone. They are not uploaded to our server. Our server stores only details about each photo so they can sync: when it was taken, your caption and tags, a weight if you add one, and a fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash) of the image file. The image cannot be recovered from that fingerprint.

Blood-work documents you choose to scan

If you upload a lab report (a PDF, JPEG or PNG) to read its values automatically, the file is sent to our server and passed to Google Cloud Vision to extract the text (see section 5). We process the file in memory and do not save it. Only the values you then review and save become part of your blood-work records.

AI assistant conversations

If you use the AI assistant, we store your messages, the assistant's replies, any actions it proposed, and technical details (which model answered and how many tokens were used). See section 5 for what is sent to the AI provider.

Preferences

Your choice of units (metric or imperial) and your default injection site.

Sign-in sessions and security data

  • When you sign in, we create a session record holding a session token, its expiry time, your IP address and your device's user agent (a short description of the app or browser).
  • If you create personal API tokens to connect another AI app (see section 5), we store each token's label, its first few characters, when it was last used, and a one-way hash of the token. We never store the full token.
  • An internal record of certain account events, such as profile changes and "clear my data" requests. We use it for security and troubleshooting.
  • The identifiers of entries you have deleted, so a phone that was offline cannot bring a deleted entry back when it syncs. These hold no health values.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID or device identifiers. We use no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, in the app or on this website. This website sets no cookies and runs no scripts.

What the app stores on your phone

  • A copy of your entries and any changes not yet synced, in a database inside the app's private storage. Each account gets its own database file.
  • Your sign-in token, in Android's secure storage, which is protected by the Android Keystore.
  • Progress photos, if you use them, in the app's private storage.

The app is set so that this data is not copied into Android cloud backups or device-to-device transfers. If you sign out and every change has synced, the app deletes that account's database from the phone. If some changes have not synced yet, it asks whether to keep them on the phone or discard them. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the phone.

Cookies

The Android app does not use cookies; it signs in with a token kept in secure storage. The companion web app uses a sign-in cookie that is required for it to work. That cookie can't be read by page scripts (it is HttpOnly), is sent only over HTTPS, and expires after 24 hours unless you keep using the app. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide PepForge: storing and syncing your entries across your devices, showing your logs, schedules, reminders, charts and estimates, and running the calculators.
  • To run your account: signing you in, keeping you signed in, and recording your age confirmation and disclaimer acceptance.
  • To provide optional features you choose to use: the AI assistant and blood-work document scanning.
  • To keep the service secure: rate limits, preventing abuse, protecting accounts and investigating problems.
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to your requests.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not build profiles for marketing, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Legal bases

We are based in Thailand, so Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA) applies to us. Where other data-protection laws, such as the EU GDPR, also apply to you, we rely on the same bases:

  • Contract: to provide the app and your account as you asked.
  • Explicit consent: for health information. You give it by choosing to enter that information, and by choosing optional features such as the AI assistant and document scanning. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting the entries, using "clear my data", or deleting your account.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the service secure and to prevent abuse.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or disclose data.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with the service providers below, who process it on our behalf to run PepForge, or where the law requires it.

ProviderWhat it doesWhat it receives

Railway, Anthropic and Google Cloud act as our processors under their data processing terms: they handle this data only to provide their service to us.

Apps you connect yourself

If your plan includes it, you can create a personal API token to let another AI app, such as ChatGPT or Claude desktop, read your PepForge records. That app then receives the data you let it read, under its own privacy policy. An app connected this way can only propose changes; nothing is saved until you confirm it in PepForge. You can revoke a token at any time.

Legal requests

We may disclose data if the law requires it, or if it is necessary to protect someone's safety. We will disclose only what is required.

6. International transfers

Our server and database are hosted by Railway in its Singapore region (asia-southeast1). If you live elsewhere, your data is transferred to and stored in Singapore. Railway is a United States company. When you use the AI assistant or document scanning, Anthropic and Google may process the relevant data in the United States or other countries. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as the providers' standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep data

  • Your account and entries: for as long as your account exists, unless you delete them first.
  • Entries you delete: body measurements, blood-work results, PCT steps, syringes, your own substances and saved templates are removed at once. Other entries (dose records, symptoms, vials, protocols, ratings, saved calculations, photo details and your own peptides) are hidden at once and permanently erased 30 days later. The delay lets every device you use catch up with the deletion.
  • "Clear my data": permanently erases all your entries, plans, blood work, body measurements, AI conversations, saved templates and API tokens at once. Your account stays, and we keep a record that the clear happened, with counts of what was removed but no health values.
  • AI conversations: kept until you delete the conversation, clear your data, or delete your account.
  • Blood-work documents sent for scanning: we do not store them.
  • Sign-in sessions: a web session expires after 24 hours without use. An app session expires after 30 days without use. Signing out of the app ends its session at once. Session records, which include the IP address and device type used to sign in, are kept until your account is deleted.
  • API tokens: erased 30 days after you revoke them, or once they expire.
  • Proposed changes from connected apps: erased 30 days after you confirm or cancel them, or after they expire.
  • Internal account event records: kept while your account exists, for security, and deleted when you delete your account (see section 9).
  • When you delete your account: see section 9.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Export it in a portable, machine-readable format.
  • Correct it if it is wrong.
  • Delete it.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time.
  • Complain to a data-protection authority: in Thailand, the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC); elsewhere, your local authority.

How to use them

  • See and correct: you can see your entries in the app and edit or delete any of them there. You can change your name, units and default injection site in the app. To change your email address, email us.
  • Delete entries: delete them one at a time in the app, or erase all of them at once with "clear my data" in the companion web app's settings. Your account is kept.
  • Export: email us from the address on your account and we will send you a copy of your account data in JSON format.
  • Delete your account: see section 9.
  • Anything else: email hello@pepforge.app from the address on your account. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting. We will reply within 30 days.

9. Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your PepForge account and everything associated with it.

  • In the app: More → Settings → Delete account. You will be asked to enter your password to confirm.
  • On the web, including if you no longer have the app: follow the steps on our account deletion page.

What is deleted: your account, email address, name, password hash, sign-in sessions, every entry, plan, blood-work result, body measurement, photo detail, AI conversation, saved template, API token and preference, and the internal event records you created. This happens immediately and in a single step, and it cannot be undone.

What is kept: if an administrator ever took an action on your account, such as changing your plan, we keep a record that the action happened, with all stored values about you removed. Photos on your phone stay on your phone until you uninstall the app.

Deactivating or suspending an account is not deletion. If an account is deactivated, its data stays until the account is deleted.

10. Adults only (18+)

PepForge is only for adults aged 18 or older. Everyone must confirm they are 18 or older before using the app. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS. The app refuses to connect to the server without it.
  • Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. Personal API tokens are stored only as SHA-256 hashes.
  • The app keeps its sign-in token in Android secure storage and keeps its data out of Android backups and device transfers.
  • Web sign-in cookies are HttpOnly and Secure. State-changing web requests are checked against an allow-list of origins.
  • Sign-in and other sensitive actions are rate-limited. Deleting your account requires your password.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the authorities where the law requires it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as PepForge changes. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. If a change is significant, for example a new service provider receiving your health data, we will also tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Paid features are not available yet. If we add paid plans through Google Play billing, we will update this policy first to describe the purchase data involved.
