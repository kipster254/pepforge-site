Your own log of what you take.

PepForge is a personal tracking tool for adults. Record your injections, oral doses and peptide doses, plan schedules and protocols, keep your blood work in one place, and see illustrative estimates built from the numbers you enter.

What it does

  • Dose logRecord injections, oral doses and peptide doses as you take them, with the details you choose to note.
  • Schedules and protocolsPlan your own schedules and see what is due today. You set every value; PepForge never suggests one.
  • Blood workKeep your lab results together so you can review them with your doctor.
  • Illustrative estimatesCurves calculated from published half-lives and the numbers you enter. They are estimates, not measurements.
  • CalculatorsUnit and concentration arithmetic on the values you type in.
  • Works offlineLog without a connection; your entries sync to your account when you are back online.

Private by design

No ads. No sale of your data. No third-party analytics or tracking. Your entries are stored on your phone and in your PepForge account so they sync, and you can delete them at any time. Read the privacy policy for exactly what is collected and where it goes.

Important

PepForge is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Nothing in it is medical advice. PepForge never recommends a dose, and nothing in it tells you to start, change or stop anything you take.

Talk to a qualified healthcare professional before starting, changing or stopping anything, and get regular blood work. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

PepForge does not sell, supply or help anyone obtain any substance. It is for adults 18 and over only.
